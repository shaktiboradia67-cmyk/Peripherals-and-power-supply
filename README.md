## Types of Printers, Scanners, and Webcams with Real-Life Applications

### Introduction
Printers, scanners, and webcams are commonly used computer peripherals. Printers produce hard copies of digital documents, scanners convert physical documents into digital formats, and webcams capture video for communication and recording.

### Comparison Table

| S. No. | Device Category | Type | Real-Life Use Case |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Printer | Inkjet Printer | Printing college assignments, notes, and color photographs. |
| 2 | Printer | Laser Printer | Printing office documents, reports, and bulk paperwork. |
| 3 | Printer | Dot Matrix Printer | Printing invoices and multi-part bills in shops. |
| 4 | Scanner | Flatbed Scanner | Scanning certificates, photographs, and important documents. |
| 5 | Scanner | Handheld Scanner | Scanning product barcodes in retail stores and supermarkets. |
| 6 | Webcam | Built-in Webcam | Attending online classes and video meetings using a laptop. |
| 7 | Webcam | External USB Webcam | Conducting video conferences and recording presentations on a desktop computer. |

### Conclusion
Printers, scanners, and webcams play an important role in everyday computing. Each type serves a specific purpose, making educational, business, and personal tasks easier and more efficient.
